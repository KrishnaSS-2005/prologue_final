About

PROLOGUE is the reading club of LBSITW. We meet to share books, argue about endings, and turn quiet reading into conversation. Whether you read one novel a year or five a month, there's a seat for you.

Vision

A campus where reading is a shared habit, not a solo chore.

Purpose

Build a friendly space for students to discover books, discuss ideas and grow as readers and speakers.

Open to all

Every branch, every year, every genre. Fiction, poetry, comics, tech and non-fiction all count.

Why join

1

Find your next book

Member-curated reading lists and a shared shelf so you never run out of good picks.

2

Speak with confidence

Discussions, debates and open mics build communication skills you'll use in interviews.

3

Meet your people

Friends across branches and batches who love the same stories you do.

4

Take part and lead

Host a session, organise an event or write for the club. Certificates for active members.
